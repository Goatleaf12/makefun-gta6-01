# Solmar Bay — GTA6-01 entry

<!-- SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT -->

An original open-world 3D game built for the make.fun **gta6-01** competition —
a sun-soaked fictional coastal city, explored on foot and by car, with traffic,
pedestrians, a wanted level, police chases, courier missions and a live minimap.

**Solmar Bay** is a city that exists only in this game. You are a freelance
courier: take jobs from the board, cross town against the clock, and stay ahead
of the Bay PD as the wanted stars climb. Nothing here is borrowed — no real
places, no names, art or music from any commercial game.
